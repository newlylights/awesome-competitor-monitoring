# Awesome Competitor Monitoring [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of awesome competitor monitoring and competitive intelligence tools, frameworks, templates, guides, and communities.

**Competitor monitoring** is the continuous tracking of competitors' digital footprints — websites, ads, search rankings, content, social media, pricing, and reviews — to inform strategic decisions.

## Books

*Foundational and modern competitive intelligence books.*

- **Competitive Strategy** — Michael E. Porter (1980) — The foundational text on competitive analysis.
- **Playing to Win** — A.G. Lafley & Roger L. Martin — Strategy framework for competitive advantage.
- **Competitive Intelligence Advantage** — Seena Sharp — Practical CI for business decisions.
- **Blue Ocean Strategy** — W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne — Creating uncontested market space.
- **Good Strategy Bad Strategy** — Richard Rumelt — Distinguishing real strategy from wishful thinking.
- **The Art of War** — Sun Tzu — Timeless competitive principles.
